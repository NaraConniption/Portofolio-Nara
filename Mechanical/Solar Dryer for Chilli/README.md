# Solar Dryer for Chilli

**Program:** Community Service (*Pengabdian Masyarakat*) | **Year:** 2025

## Overview
Designed an efficient solar dryer tailored for local chili farmers to accelerate the drying process, improve hygiene, and reduce post-harvest losses.

## Key Features & Contributions
* **Thermal & Mechanical Design:** Developed the physical structure and airflow mechanism of the solar dryer optimized for agricultural use.
* **Community Service Implementation:** Designed and delivered the unit directly to local farmers to enhance post-harvest quality and economic value.

# SINALA – Floating Photovoltaic (FPV) Energy System for Aquaculture

**Role:** Research Assistant | 2025 – 2026

## Overview
SINALA is a 1.1 kWp floating photovoltaic (FPV) energy system developed in collaboration with **PT ORELA Shipyard** under the leadership of **Dr. Erna Septyaningrum**. The system is designed to be rented by local fishermen to provide reliable power for floating fish cages (*keramba apung*).

---

## Key Responsibilities & Contributions
* **System Co-Development:** Contributed to the design and development of the 1.1 kWp floating PV system tailored for aquaculture applications.
* **Electrical Box Design:** Designed the IP66-rated electrical panel box for safe marine and outdoor deployment, including:
  * Single Line Diagram (SLD)
  * Installation and layout plan
